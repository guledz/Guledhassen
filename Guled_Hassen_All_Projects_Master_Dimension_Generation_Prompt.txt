IMAGE COMPOSITION RULES

For each project:
- Keep important content inside a safe central area.
- Avoid placing critical text close to crop edges.
- Maintain consistent margins.
- Use the same visual hierarchy across all projects.
- The project itself must dominate the image.
- Do not overfill the frame.
- Use realistic UI/product screenshots when actual project material is available.
- Never fabricate screenshots and present them as real project evidence.
- For concept visuals, clearly treat them as concept/mockup material.
