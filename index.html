02 — Services

What I can do for a business.

Four areas of work. Each one starts from a business problem, not a deliverable list.

  1. 01

    Brand Identity

    For businesses whose identity is inconsistent across signage, print and screen. Logo systems, colour, typography and application rules that hold together everywhere the brand appears.

  2. 02

    Websites & Digital Catalogs

    For businesses with a real product range but no way to show it. Filterable catalogues, product structure, company profiles and enquiry paths that turn browsing into a conversation.

  3. 03

    Digital Products & Tools

    For work that lives in spreadsheets, folders and memory. Dashboards, registries and small utilities that make information findable and comparable.

  4. 04

    Social Media & Business Presentation

    For businesses that are seen before they are visited. Post and campaign layouts, profile systems and presentation decks that carry the same identity as the website.

03 — About

Harar-born. Engineer-trained. Built for the web.

The background behind the work.

I was born in Harar, Ethiopia in 1994 and trained as an engineer, graduating in 2016. Since then I have worked as a contractor, run a bakery business and worked in a municipality office — three very different organisations with the same recurring problem: information that is hard to find, hard to compare and hard to trust.

That is the thread running through my design work. I build brand identities, websites, digital catalogues and small tools that make a business easier to understand and easier to buy from. Most of my projects are for Ethiopian businesses — furniture, coffee, medical supply, construction, hospitality — and most of them start the same way: a business that works well offline and is almost invisible online.

I work end to end. Structure first, then interface, then build. Everything I ship is responsive, hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and built so it can be extended later without a rewrite.
