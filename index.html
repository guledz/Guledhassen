04 — About / 04

Harar-born.
Engineer-trained.
Built for the web.

Where I come from, the work I have done, and what I care about building.

I was born in Harar, Ethiopia, in 1994 and grew up in the city. A single parent raised me and sent me to private school, and I am grateful that both of my parents are alive today.

I graduated as an engineer in 2016. Since then I have worked as a contractor, run a bakery business and worked in a municipality office. Each one showed me how real organisations operate, and where a better system would make the work easier.

I am married and a father of three, which keeps my work practical and my priorities clear. What I am most passionate about is technology and digital experience: building websites, business platforms and data tools that make information clear and easy to use.
